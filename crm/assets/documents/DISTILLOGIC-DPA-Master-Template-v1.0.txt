4. DEFINITIONS
For purposes of this DPA:

“Personal Data”
means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

“Processing”
means any operation performed on Personal Data, including:

collection;

recording;

organisation;

storage;

alteration;

retrieval;

consultation;

use;

transmission;

disclosure;

restriction;

deletion;

destruction.

“Data Subject”
means the natural person to whom Personal Data relates.

“Controller”
means the entity determining the purposes and means of Processing.

“Processor”
means the entity Processing Personal Data on behalf of the Controller.

“Subprocessor”
means a third party engaged by DISTILLOGIC to Process Personal Data on behalf of the Client.

“Personal Data Breach”
means a breach of security leading to accidental or unlawful:

destruction;

loss;

alteration;

unauthorised disclosure of;

or access to Personal Data.

“Applicable Data Protection Law”
includes Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and other applicable data-protection legislation.

22. PERSONAL DATA BREACH
DISTILLOGIC shall notify the Client without undue delay after becoming aware of a confirmed Personal Data Breach affecting Personal Data Processed under this DPA.

DISTILLOGIC shall provide information reasonably available concerning:

nature of the incident;

affected systems;

categories of Personal Data;

categories of affected Data Subjects;

likely consequences;

containment measures;

mitigation actions.

Information may be provided in phases where it is not immediately available.

23. BREACH INVESTIGATION
Following a Personal Data Breach, DISTILLOGIC shall:

take reasonable steps to contain the incident;

investigate the cause;

mitigate reasonably foreseeable harm;

preserve appropriate incident records;

cooperate reasonably with the Client.

DISTILLOGIC shall not make public statements identifying the Client without authorisation unless legally required.

The Client may conduct or commission a reasonable audit where necessary to verify compliance with this DPA.

Audits shall normally:

be conducted during normal business hours;

be subject to reasonable prior notice;

avoid unnecessary disruption;

be limited to systems and information relevant to the Client;

be subject to confidentiality requirements.

Where an independent audit report or certification reasonably satisfies the Client's requirements, the Parties should consider using such evidence before requiring an onsite audit.

29. AUDIT COSTS
Each Party shall normally bear its own internal costs relating to routine compliance activity.

Where the Client requests an exceptional, extensive or repeated audit not arising from a material DISTILLOGIC breach, reasonable additional assistance may be chargeable subject to prior agreement.

Binding Corporate Rules;

other legally recognised transfer mechanisms.

technical supplementary measures;

contractual safeguards.

notify the Client;

review the legal validity of the request;

limit disclosure to what is legally required;

seek appropriate confidentiality where reasonably available.

DISTILLOGIC shall not voluntarily provide Client Personal Data to public authorities except where authorised or legally required.
